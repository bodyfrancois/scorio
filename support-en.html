Frequently asked questions

My games/scores disappeared, what happened?
Scorup stores all your data only on your device. If you uninstalled the app, switched phones without transferring data, or reset your device, your local history is gone — we have no backup copy on our end, since the app collects nothing on our servers (see our privacy policy).

How can I request a new game?
Email us at the address above with the game's name and, if possible, a link to its official rules.

Is the app paid?
No, Scorup is free and ad-free. An optional support button (free donation) is available in the app for anyone who'd like to support its development.
