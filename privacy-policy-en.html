Privacy Policy — Scorup

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Scorup mobile app (the "App"), published by BODY François (the "Publisher"), available on the App Store and Google Play Store.

1. Summary

Scorup does not collect, store on a server, transmit, or sell any personal data. All information you enter stays on your device only.

2. What data is processed

The App lets you create games, add players, and track scores. This information (player names, scores, game history, statistics, display preferences) is:

  • stored exclusively locally, on your device, using the operating system's native storage mechanism (AsyncStorage);
  • never transmitted to the Publisher, to a remote server, or to any third party;
  • accessible only by you, from your device.

The Publisher has no technical access to any of this data and cannot view, modify, or retrieve it.

3. No account, no network connection required

Scorup works entirely offline. The App does not require account creation and makes no network calls to function.

4. Advertising, trackers and third-party services

The App contains:

  • no advertising;
  • no advertising tracker or analytics SDK (Google Analytics, Meta SDK, etc.);
  • no in-app purchase service;
  • no data sharing with third parties, since no data is collected.

5. Permissions requested on your device

The App does not request any special permission (no access to camera, location, contacts, microphone, or photos) beyond what is strictly necessary for its basic operation.

6. Use by minors

Scorup is a family-friendly app, usable by any age in the context of board games. Since no data is collected or transmitted, the App presents no specific risk to minors' data and, by virtue of this complete absence of collection, meets applicable requirements (GDPR, COPPA).

7. Your rights

Since your data stays on your device and is never transmitted to the Publisher, you keep full control over it: you can view, modify, or delete it directly from the App, or by uninstalling the App (which removes all locally stored data).

For any question about this policy, you can contact the Publisher using the details below.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the App evolves (addition of advertising, usage statistics, online sync, etc.). In case of a significant change in the nature of the data processed, this page will be updated and the last-modified date at the top will be refreshed. We encourage you to check it periodically.

9. Contact

For any question about this privacy policy or the App:

Email: scorup.support@gmail.com
